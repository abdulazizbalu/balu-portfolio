how it goes

  • discover: One call, then a short written brief: who uses it, what done looks like, what can break.
  • spec: A protocol before code: data, rules, acceptance criteria and test cases agreed up front.
  • build: I direct AI coding agents and review every diff. Fast iterations, small PRs, nothing unreviewed.
  • verify: Tests, evals and a reference to compare against. Results are reproducible.
  • ship: Deploy on Vercel or Railway, add monitoring, hand over docs and a runbook.

questions

How do we agree on scope?

After a 20-minute call and a short written brief you get a fixed scope and a date, so the plan does not grow on its own.

How long does it take?

A bot or a site usually takes 1–2 weeks, an AI feature 2–4 weeks, a web app or MVP 4–8 weeks. You see a working version early and then every week.

You do. At handover you get the repository, documentation, a runbook and tests, deployed to your accounts.

Yes. Most of my paid work is under NDA, which is why client names on this site are hidden.
