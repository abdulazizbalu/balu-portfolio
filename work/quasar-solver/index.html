quasar-solver

Quantum-inspired optimizers are often sold on demos. quasar-solver asks a plain question: how far from the proven optimum does QUBO annealing land, with honest confidence intervals?

100runs per size
20cities, proven optimum
86.7% → 3.7%gap after swap moves
95%confidence intervals

Setup

Each size has ten seeded Euclidean instances and ten solver seeds. The reference for every instance, including 15 and 20 cities, is the proven Held-Karp optimum. All methods get the same budget of attempted operations.

Results

  • Bit-flip QUBO annealing reaches an 86.65% mean gap at 20 cities: one-hot penalty landscapes are hard to search.
  • A permutation-preserving swap move brings the gap down to 3.69%, still 3.32 points above multi-start 2-opt at 0.37%.
  • At 10 cities swap annealing is slightly ahead of 2-opt (0.27% vs 0.41%), a small difference in these runs.

What is inside

  • QUBO builder and simulated annealing solver
  • TSP, capacitated VRP and VRP with time windows, with independent feasibility checks
  • OR-Tools reference solvers
  • Interactive route visualization demo
