// Local-first AI desktop app · 2026

Nur

Many offices cannot paste documents into a cloud chatbot. Nur brings the useful part of an AI assistant onto the laptop: the model, the history and the text all stay on the machine.

# Features

  • AI tasks on local Gemma 4: chat with history, email drafts by tone and language, summaries, RU ↔ UZ (Latin and Cyrillic) ↔ EN translation, seven rewrite modes
  • Offline utilities: unit, currency and file-size converters, numbers in words in Russian and Uzbek, transliteration
  • First-run wizard that installs Ollama and downloads the model, with a skip-and-install-later path for offline users

# Architecture

Electron main process with typed IPC handlers and an Ollama HTTP client; a preload bridge with a typed API; a React 19 renderer split into views, shared components and a useGemma hook; validation for every IPC payload.
